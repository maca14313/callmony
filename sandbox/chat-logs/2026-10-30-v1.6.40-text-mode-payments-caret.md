## Item 1 — text-mode numbers are annotations, not math

The user's rule: a number typed while the calculator is in TEXT mode
(the system QWERTY) must not be calculated; keypad numbers are the math.
Implemented by tracking WHICH characters of the active line came from
the text keyboard: `qwertyIdx`, a set of character indices maintained by
ONE edit funnel (`onCalcEdited`) — every mutation of the line (QWERTY
keystroke, keypad key, native paste/cut, the ± re-serialize, MR) flows
through it, and a prefix/suffix diff keeps the marks alive through
arbitrary edits (untouched characters keep their marks, the retyped
middle region is text-keyboard only when the change came from the
QWERTY).

At parse time `quoteTextNumbers(calcText, qwertyIdx)` wraps any
number-carrying-text-keyboard-characters token in double quotes, and the
grammar (`parseCalcNote`) treats a quoted token as a WORD — never a
number. The marker persists through the save (a saved note reads
`"2" rooms 500` — the 500 is math, the quoted 2 is an annotation), so a
reopened note keeps the split; old unquoted notes still calculate
exactly as before. A line whose every number is quoted (or that has
none) is a PURE ANNOTATION: its text rides `CalcNote.text`, commits via
Next line as an annotation line, round-trips through the ledger, and
never enters any total.

## Item 2 — the TOTAL moves under the ledger and grows a payments modal

The TOTAL bar left the top of the canvas: it now sits AFTER the last
calculation entry (the active line, its live result and the steps), on
the paper itself — no card background, no border, just ink. Tap or
long-press opens THE SHARED PAYMENTS MODAL — the same one every
individual line total opens now: the ORIGINAL amount of the calculation
as the headline, the recorded payments listed with the NEW AMOUNT after
each one ("the new amount history"), the paid · remaining summary, and
Make payment / Copy actions.

Make payment finally REDUCES visibly: the payment sheet (which the
modal opens over the still-open editor, saving the entry first) records
a real entry-level payment — `Payment.lineIdx` attributes the ones made
from a line's modal to that line — and the editor's total grows a
"Paid 300 Br · Remaining 0 Br" line the moment the payment lands; a
paid line shows its remaining total plus a tiny "Paid x" underneath.
Some buttons after the totals, per the spec: a one-tap
"Make payment · <remaining>" chip and a "Payments · n" chip that opens
the modal. Deleting or unlocking a line orphans its payments back to
the entry level (the money stays recorded — it lists under the total).

## Items 3-5 — full locked lines, the confirm modals, the lock pill

Locked lines wrap: the full calculation text, no single-line clamp.
Removing a line goes through the app's custom confirmation modal
(Confirm.ask — never a native dialog), and the invisible phantom button
the user kept hitting beside the Aa/123 toggle is replaced by a VISIBLE
red ✕ chip right beside the toggle that clears the active line through
the same confirmation. The lock is a NOTICEABLE 30dp brand-tinted pill
with a border — tempting to press — and long-pressing it (or the row)
unlocks the line.

## Item 6 — the caret sits at the right place

The text-mode field is a `TextFieldValue` now, with the selection
remembered across the Aa ↔ 123 swaps: toggling from number to text puts
the blinking caret at the END of the line (where writing continues),
not before the first word; Next line resets it to the start of the
fresh line and re-arms focus; unlocking lands it at the end. The toggle
buttons are 30dp tall — the same height as Save and Next line.

## Item 7 — Save keeps the editor open

`save()` no longer pops the editor: the first Save of a brand-new entry
creates it (and remembers the created id — no duplicate entries on
later saves), a toast confirms, and from then on the v1.6.38 autosave
owns every change: 600ms after the last keystroke and again on close.
Make payment from the modal uses the same live-entry flow.
