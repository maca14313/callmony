## Decode + fix map

### Report 1 — "make the history icon more visible"

Two history icons exist in the app, and BOTH were easy to miss:

**THE CALCULATOR** (`ui/components/Calculator.kt`): the history toggle in the
sheet header was a bare 22dp glyph in the subdued `C.sub` tone — three naked
faint icons (Σ, history, copy) floated on the canvas with no background, no
border, no label. FIX: the history control is now a **labelled brand pill** —
icon + bold "History" text (`calc.history`), brand-soft fill with a brand
hairline, and it inverts to solid brand while the panel is open. The Σ
(advanced) and copy controls beside it got real chips too (34dp cardAlt
rounded squares with hairline borders; Σ flips to brand-soft + brand border
when active), so nothing in the calculator header is a naked faint icon
anymore.

**CONTACT DETAIL** (`ui/overlays/ContactDetail.kt`): the history button that
rides in the total/settled balance row (v1.6.27) was a bare 18dp glyph in a
transparent 32dp box — no background, no border — it read as decoration, not
as the button that jumps to the Info tab (whose first block is the contact's
full call history). FIX: a **34dp brand-soft circular chip with a brand
hairline and a 20dp glyph** — a visibly tappable button, consistent with the
calculator's new chip language.

### Report 2 — "the Ledger quick item (a label + price) should be in the credit, debit, expenses and income, not in the outer ledger"

The v1.6.27 quick item rendered in the OUTER ledger (always, under the
"Single entries" header) and GUESSED a kind when no kind tab was active
("defaulting to Credit on a contact and Expense in the general ledger" —
exactly what the user did not want). FIX
(`ui/sections/LedgerSection.kt`): the `[Item label][Price][＋]` row now
renders **ONLY while a KIND tab is active** — Credit / Debit on a contact
ledger, Income / Expense on the general ledger — placed AFTER the filter
strip (inside the kind view). Since the kind is no longer a guess, the ＋
always books the entry AS THE TAB BEING VIEWED. The outer/neutral view
(All / Unpaid / Overdue) stays clean, exactly as asked. Locked with a new
Robolectric test (`QuickItemKindTabsTest`): the quick row must NOT exist on
All, must appear on the Income tab, and must disappear again when returning
to All.

### Report 3 — "Press feedback is not there for the input … all the inputs should have [it]" (+ "check github, give me apk and push even the apk")

The v1.6.25 PressIndication rides `LocalIndication`, which only the
`clickable`-family consults — **a BasicTextField never asks for an
indication, so tapping ANY text field showed zero reaction** (the quantity /
unit / price inputs in the items ledger are Fields; the hero amount, the
search boxes, and the quick-item row are raw BasicTextFields). FIX: new
`Modifier.pressVeil(corner)` in `ui/kit/Kit.kt` — a self-contained OBSERVER
for an input's own box: it watches pointers over its bounds WITHOUT ever
consuming them (the field keeps every gesture — caret placement, long-press
selection, paste menu, IME), and paints the same 16% press veil while a
finger is down (rounded to the box's own corner radius), then fades it out.
Freeze-lesson compliant by construction: every await in the gesture loop
suspends on a real event (no busy-spin — the v1.6.24 autolock lesson) and
the pressed flag is written exactly once per gesture boundary (the
snapshot-write lesson). Applied to EVERY input surface:

- `Field` (the shared boxed input — covers the items-ledger quantity / unit
  / price fields and every editor: contact editor, lists, notes, reminders,
  settings, onboarding currency…)
- the entry editor's HERO AMOUNT box
- the QUICK ITEM label/price inputs
- the CONTACT PICKER search box
- the dominant SEARCH screen's box
- the ONBOARDING country search box
- the KEYPAD's number display (a real input — paste/cut live there)

`PickerField` already had press feedback through its `clickable`.
