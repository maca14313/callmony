## What shipped

1. **The currency beside every total is TAPPABLE** — a tiny chip prints the currency each total renders in (the line's own choice → the entry's sum currency → the live setting); the tap opens the scoped picker. The sum's chip writes `LedgerEntry.calcCurrency`; a committed line's chip rewrites that line's currency (riding the save as the `⏎`-style ` ~USD` marker); the active line's chip rides its commit.
2. **Copy and ✕ live ON the individual total's row** — the line's total, its currency chip, its paid hint, its date, its lock glyph, its copy and its delete all share one row; the active line's copy sits on its live-total row.
3. **The total's size, the date's size, the left rail** — the individual total renders at the line TEXT's own size in every state (locked, unlocked, editing — the font-size choice scales them together); the live total indents past the prefix column so it aligns with the field above it (the pushed-left fix); the date renders 11sp Normal (smaller and lighter); the ledger column moved in — the text starts ~32dp from the screen edge, was ~64dp.
4. **The compact sum row + the ring counts what is PAID** — the ring shrank 40dp → 30dp, the double padding bands are gone, and the circle counts the PAID share now: 0% with nothing paid (the old one counted what was LEFT, so a fresh entry read "100 with no any payment"), green at 100%, matching the entry cards' own rings.
5. **The break survives the lock** — the enter key's newline rides the saved grammar as the ⏎ marker (U+23CE — nothing a keyboard can type), the parse stamps the token after it, and the display puts the visual line break back: a calculation typed across several lines STAYS several lines when it locks, through the save, and on the reopen. The ledger card's preview folds the marker to a space.
6. **The title under the header + the font-size icon on the header** — the title input is a full-width row of its own beneath the 38dp header band; the new Aa chip on the header opens the S / M / L picker (16/13sp, 18/14sp, 20/16sp), scaling the active line, the committed lines and every total; the choice rides the entry as `calcFontSize`.
7. **The edit-entry page is GONE** — the whole form is deleted; the canvas IS the editor (✕ or back closes it; back with the keypad up closes the keypad first). Every field the form used to carry seeds once from the existing row and rides along through every save; the items data survives untouched.
8. **The contact's ＋ asks the kind** — the ＋ Credit / ＋ Debit pills (and the whole action stack beside them) are gone from the contact detail page; the ledger FAB's — and the header row's — ＋ opens a compact ＋ Credit / ＋ Debit chooser, and each choice opens the canvas with that kind preset. The General ledger keeps the direct new-canvas behavior. (Round two: the chooser is a ROOT-LEVEL host sheet carrying its ownerId explicitly — the first cut's remember-state bridge cross-wired through the shell's shared RootFab slot, and an in-place sheet sat below the viewport inside the contact page's tall scroll.)
9. **The cards show most of the calculation** — every entry card previews its whole ledger, up to four lines.
10. **Payments update** — tapping a recorded payment in the payments modal reopens the SAME compact sheet as an UPDATE form, prefilled with the amount / date / method / note; the save rewrites it IN PLACE (`AppStore.updatePayment` keeps the id, the line attribution and the auto marker; the cap accounts for the payment being edited).
