## Where we were

GitHub checked first: origin/main == ea93a51 (v1.6.40, versionCode 51,
171/171 tests). The sandbox had reset again — local tree resynced to
origin/main and the Android SDK reinstalled before anything could build.

## Item 1 — the caret, always

A focus TOKEN (`focusToken`) bumps on every caret-worthy event — the
canvas opening (it starts at 1) or raising from the collapsed band, the
Aa / 123 toggle, Next line, an unlock. Each bump re-focuses the active
field of the CURRENT mode: the QWERTY field through a double Compose
focus request (immediately, then again 200 ms later to beat the
focus-clears that OverlayHost / HostSheetsHost fire when their stacks
change), the number field through a new `focusToken` param on
NoImeNumberField — the Android actual makes the EditText request REAL
VIEW focus (twice, same pattern) with the IME still suppressed by
setShowSoftInputOnFocus(false), so the caret blinks in number mode with
no keyboard ever. The old wantTextFocus flag is gone; closeKeypad (the
back-button path) deliberately stays quiet — no caret, both keyboards
gone, exactly as specified in v1.6.39.

## Items 2+3 — the keypad grid

The = key is removed (the result zone still opens by typing " = " in
text mode). The ⌫ moved into ÷'s old top-right place, ÷ dropped one row,
and the freed first-row slot went to a WIDE % key:

    AC   [    %    ]  ⌫
     7    8    9    ÷
     4    5    6    ×
     1    2    3    −
     ±    0    .    +

— the classic phone-calculator operator column, exactly "the x icon
that deletes numbers should be in the place of ÷ the ÷ should get down
on row".

## Item 4 — text-mode colour

Everything written on the TEXT keyboard renders in the calculator's blue
(0xFF4A7D9B — the %/± hue), everything from the keypad stays ink. Live
in the active line: a VisualTransformation colouring the QWERTY field's
runs, ForegroundColorSpans over the native EditText in number mode (a
new `highlight`/`highlightColor` param — spans rebuilt on every
programmatic sync, cursor kept at the end). Persisted on the locked
lines: `coloredLockedLine` paints exactly the QUOTED tokens (the v1.6.40
text-mode markers) in the blue over the base colour — pure and
harness-pinned.

## Item 5 — the keypad covers the bottom navigation

The app's standing rule since v1.6.26 was "nothing renders under the
nav". The system IME never obeyed it — and now the calculator's number
keypad doesn't either, per the explicit request. A new ROOT-LEVEL
OVERLAY SLOT (`RootBottomOverlays` in Kit) renders in CallmonyApp AFTER
the bottom nav (the only z-position that can cover it — the onboarding
wizard always lived there). While number mode is armed the editor pushes
the keypad into that slot — a transparent full-size Box that never
consumes input, with the 224dp keypad anchored BottomCenter. The canvas
measures its own bottom band (the imeLiftPadding position trick) and
reserves exactly the strip the keypad covers, so the controls row sits
right on the keypad's top edge; the Toaster lifts by the same amount so
toasts stay visible. The keypad hides while the payments modal or any
host sheet is open (nothing floats under it), and the native back button
still closes it first.

## Items 6+11 — totals below, pay chips, history on the canvas

A locked line's own total moved BELOW its calculation (a Column under
the wrapping text) with a compact PAY chip beside it; the sum total
carries one too. THE PAYMENT HISTORY renders on the canvas itself for
every entry and the sum — one tight row per payment: date · − amount ·
the NOTE · → the new amount it left — under each paid line total and
under the grand total, every row tappable into the payments modal. The
modal was rebuilt COMPACT: one summary line (original · paid ·
remaining), tight history rows, one action row (Pay · remaining /
Copy).

## Item 7 — the payment cap

openPaymentSheet gained `maxAmount`: every payment made from the
calculator (line or total) is validated against it — anything above the
remaining is refused with a localized toast, nothing recorded. The
modal's Pay action passes the remaining; the sheet's Save enforces it.

## Items 8+9 — the footer and the header

The DELETE-ENTRY button left the editor footer (Save alone — entries
are deleted from the ledger list, never mid-edit). The canvas header
shrank to a 38dp band with 32/28dp controls and a small title, carrying
UNDO / REDO beside the collapse chevron: a snapshot of the whole
calculator editing state (lines + active text + QWERTY marks) rides the
undo stack before every mutation — every funnel keystroke, commit,
unlock, delete, clear — and undo ↔ redo walk the two stacks (60 deep,
redo cleared by any new edit).

## Item 10 — right to left

The canvas, the root keypad and the payments modal all wrap in
CompositionLocalProvider(LocalLayoutDirection provides …) based on the
language's rtl flag (Arabic): rows mirror, the keypad grid flips, start
alignments swap. Expressions still read naturally (digits are LTR runs
under bidi).

## Tests

181/181 (was 171): CalcNoteTest +1 (the colour spans); the NEW
EntryEditCalcV1641Test 9-battery; EntryEditCalcV1640Test updated for the
compact modal's Pay label.

Three harness lessons this round, all worth keeping: (1) Robolectric
reuses ONE sandbox across same-config tests — a leaked open payment
sheet keeps HostSheets non-empty, which keeps the root keypad DOWN and
its scrim swallows taps in every later test, and a leaked
language="ar" makes every later test read Arabic — V1641's @After now
clears the global hosts and resets the language; (2) the v1.6.39
placeholder contains "ball", so an empty-field hasText probe must use a
word that is not in the placeholder; (3) onGloballyPositioned placed
AFTER padding reports the inset coordinates and oscillates against the
padding it feeds — the nav-band probe lives OUTSIDE the paddings (the
same reason imeLiftPadding measures where it does). The one-off
RecentsV1636 flake in an intermediate run was the documented
sandbox-load flakiness — two forced clean full-suite runs came back
181/181.
