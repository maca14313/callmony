## 1 — the number-mode lag/close

A Robolectric stress battery (300 rapid keypad keystrokes, then 50 more
after 25 committed lines) came back FLAT — 2 ms per key, no degradation,
no exception. The Compose layer is innocent; what the harness cannot
emulate is the native field's IMM interplay on a real device. The
v1.6.41 caret work left three real hazards there, all removed:

- the EditText fired UNCONDITIONAL `requestFocus()` twice per token bump
  (0 ms + 220 ms) — on a real Samsung/OneUI build every redundant focus
  request can bounce the InputMethodManager (restartInput churn → the
  reported lag → the ANR "close"). Focus is now requested only when the
  view really lacks it; the delayed catch-up still covers the
  pre-attachment no-op, but it too fires only if still unfocused.
- the AndroidView `update` block rewrote `textSize`/`textColor` on EVERY
  recomposition — each call a `requestLayout()` on every keystroke. Both
  are cached against the last applied value now: a no-op recomposition
  touches the view zero times.
- the autosave key serialized the whole ledger on EVERY keystroke even
  for a brand-new, not-yet-saved entry. It is only computed once the
  entry actually exists (`currentEntry != null`).

The stress battery lives on as a regression test
(`rapidNumberModeTypingStaysFastAndAlive`).

## 2 — the placeholder WAS the caret bug

The QWERTY field's decorationBox REPLACED the field with the example
text whenever the line was empty — after Next line the fresh line showed
"ball 100 x 2 …" INSTEAD of the caret (the field was not composed at
all). The placeholder and hint are gone; the field always renders (caret
included) with a stable 44 dp write area. The v1.6.41 focus token keeps
canvas open / toggle / Next line / unlock all re-focusing as before.

## 5 — RTL from the first typed word

The num field (native EditText) always resolved Arabic bidi per
paragraph; the Compose field inherited the layout direction, so the
first Arabic words in TEXT mode ordered LTR. `textDirection = Content`
on the field's text style: the first strong character sets the
paragraph direction — Arabic starts from the right end, exactly like the
number field.

## 6+8 — the sum reads out; the entries own the money

- The SUM total: no pay chip, no history rows, no tap/long-press modal —
  the total, plus the Paid · Remaining line once anything is paid.
- Each individual entry: LOCKED by default (pay chip greyed + inert,
  total inert); TAPPING the lock pill UNLOCKS the entry — green
  open-padlock pill, brand-tinted block, armed chip. Long-press on the
  pill or the row still pulls the line into the active field for editing
  (unchanged v1.6.39 behaviour).
- The entry's payment history renders ABOVE its total as a list, headed
  by the lock state (Locked / Unlocked, each in its own colour) with a
  hairline separating history from total; every row shows the FULL note
  (wraps, never ellipsizes), the date, the amount and the new amount it
  left.

## 10 — dates

render their dates (as before).

Two harness lessons this round, both semantics-merging traps:
1. A testTag on a NON-CLICKABLE node merges into a big ancestor —
   `performClick` on it taps the ANCESTOR'S CENTRE (a "dead" total tap
   actually landed on the lock pill and toggled it, poisoning the rest
   of the test). Inertness is asserted by probing
   `SemanticsActions.OnClick` instead of tapping.
2. A DISABLED chip's tag rides the row's merged node (its own clickable
   boundary vanishes), so the locked chip is proven by what does NOT
   open — and the date Text merges into the line, so dates are probed
   by TEXT (short month names: "Oct 31, 2026"), not by tag.
