## The "ball" bug — why the text disappeared

v1.6.38 kept the active line in TWO places: `calcText` (the QWERTY
field) and `calcNote` (the parsed tokens). Switching Aa → 123 hid the
field and showed the token flow; switching back ran
`calcText = serializeCalcNote(calcNote)` — and `parseCalcNote("ball")`
produces ZERO tokens (words only wait for a number; `flush()` drops
them), so the serialization came back EMPTY and "ball" was gone. The
whole split model was the problem.

**The fix is the redesign:** `calcText` is now THE single source of
truth; `calcNote`/tokens/total/steps are all derived from it live. The
active line is ONE native editable field in BOTH modes (the QWERTY
`BasicTextField` in text mode, the IME-suppressed native
`NoImeNumberField` in num mode — alignStart + maxLines params added),
so a mode switch just swaps the input method and the text carries over
untouched. The keypad appends to the text (with an auto-space after a
word, so "ball" + 2 0 0 becomes "ball 200" and the label sticks).

## What shipped (annotated calculation)

1. **Text / number / Next line / Save — ON the canvas, on the line
   under the active one, and SMALL** (the spec's own words): a tiny
   Aa/123 segmented pill, a 30dp Next-line pill, a 30dp Save pill. The
   footer's Save/Delete stay for the collapsed form. The keypad's ⏎
   slot became an `=` key (opens the "= total 500" result zone).
2. **The "ball" bug** — fixed by the unified line (above).
3. **The icons on the numbers are GONE**, and so is the icon-click
   interaction entirely: no tag badges, no clickable numbers, no
   operator cycling, no label popover (all documented in
   `removed/2026-10-30-canvas-tag-badges-label-popover.md`). Labels are
   typed.
4. **The canvas is FULL SCREEN over every thing in the editor** — a
   `fillMaxSize` surface rendered after the Sheet (header, pinned
   bands, form and footer all sit under it; the app's standing v1.6.26
   rule "the bottom nav is always visible" is respected, so the canvas
   owns the content area). A ✕/chevron-down collapses it to a compact
   band in the form that raises it again.
5. **The keypad is smaller and compact**: a 178–224dp band (was
   172–256), 5dp gaps, 14dp side padding — digits stay 24sp.
6. **The native back button closes the keypad** — and then collapses
   the canvas, then closes the editor (the AppBackHandler chain:
   canvas handler composes after the Sheet's, so it wins while up).
7. **Long-press a locked line UNLOCKS it** for editing: the line comes
   back down into the active field (whatever was being typed commits
   first, so nothing is lost).
8. **Individual line totals share the last total's features**:
   long-press a line's own total → the same menu (Make payment · that
   total / Copy it).
9. **The unlocked line has native copy/paste/cut + selection handles**
   — it is a real text field in BOTH modes now (in num mode it is the
   same EditText trick the dialer uses: editable, IME suppressed).
10. **Every calculation line has a copy icon, locked or unlocked** —
    `CopyBtn` (`calcLineCopy:$i`, `calcActiveCopy`) beside every line.
