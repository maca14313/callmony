## The decode + the fixes

**#1 — "sometimes the regular native keyboard shows up on the numbers
mode":** the QWERTY field in TEXT mode is served by the system IME;
toggling Aa → 123 removes the field from composition, but nothing
deterministically tells the IME to go away — the toggle's ime.hide()
races the field's disposal, returning to the app re-shows the keyboard
for the last served editor, and OEM builds keep it around on focus
change. That is exactly "sometimes". The number field never wants the
soft keyboard under ANY circumstance, so the fix is at the view level
where the leak lives: `NoImeEditText` force-hides the IME on EVERY focus
gain (`onFocusChanged(true)`) and EVERY window-focus gain
(`onWindowFocusChanged(true)`), exception-proof, plus the toggle now
clears the QWERTY focus FIRST (`focusMgr.clearFocus()` before the mode
flip) so the IME loses its served editor before the field dies.

**#2 — payments moved to the sum total, with the circle:** the per-line
PAY chips, the per-line on-canvas history blocks and the tap-a-line-total
shortcuts are all removed. The sum total row carries:
- THE PAY BUTTON — always armed (even fully paid: the chip is the
  HISTORY's entry point too — "the history of the payment also should
  show when the payment btn is clicked on the sum total"); the modal's
  own Pay action hides when nothing remains.
- THE LEFT CIRCLE — a 36dp ring filled with the paid fraction (green
  when fully paid) carrying the count of still-unpaid lines in its
  centre + an "N left" caption. The count settles lines FIFO
  (`calcLeftCount` in Calc.kt): payments ATTRIBUTED to a line (the
  pre-v1.6.45 lineIdx ones) spend on that line alone; ENTRY-level
  payments (everything recorded now) walk the ledger oldest-first — so
  the circle reads 2 → 1 → 0 exactly as a real ledger gets paid off. The
  first draft double-counted attributed payments through the FIFO walk —
  caught by the pure test battery before any device ever saw it.
- THE MODAL — one summary line (original · paid · remaining), the
  history list with the FULL note (wrapping, never ellipsized), each
  payment's date and what it left, Pay (prefilled with the remaining,
  capped) and Copy (the whole-ledger export, #6).

**#3 — the colour separation, always:** new pure `textModeRanges()` — a
token is TEXT-mode content when QUOTED (the persisted marker of a QWERTY
number) or when it CONTAINS A LETTER (letters only ever come from the
text keyboard; the numeric keypad has none). That one rule drives BOTH
renderers: `coloredLockedLine` (locked lines — words coloured now, not
just quoted numbers) and the qwerty marks REBUILT in `unlockCalcLine`
when a line is long-pressed back into the active field — so "rooms" is
blue and "500" is ink live, committed, saved, reloaded and re-edited.

**#4 — the compact record-payment sheet:** the hint paragraph and the
kind · amount headline are gone (the title carries the amount, one tiny
line carries the remaining), fill-remaining moved INSIDE the amount
field, Date + Note share one row, the five method segments became
compact chips on a scroller, Save is one small full-width button. Four
tight rows where there were seven stacked blocks; the amount arrives
prefilled, so the common case is one tap.

**#5 — dates on everything:** every line's commit date (v1.6.42) and
every payment's date render on the canvas hints, in the modal list, in
the record sheet and in the export.

**#6 — copies include the totals:** a line's copy carries its own total
("rooms 500 = 500"), the active line's copy carries the live running
total, and the sum total gained its own Copy button exporting EVERYTHING
(`formatCalcLedgerExport`): lines with dates and totals, attributed AND
entry-level payments (date · amount · full note · what it left), the
items sum, the TOTAL, Paid · Remaining. The modal's Copy exports the
same.
