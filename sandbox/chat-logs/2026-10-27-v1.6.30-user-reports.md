   slower, just remove it" → the splash pane, its exit fade and the process
   flags are deleted entirely; the shell composes from frame one.
2. **Full number in the numbers strip** — the long-press sheet's number
   tiles no longer ellipsize at a fixed 196dp; they grow to the full
   pretty-printed number.
3. **Contact-detail icon after the name** — headerRight slot on the
   long-press sheet: account-circle icon right after the person's name,
   opens the contact's detail page (saved contacts only).
4. **Calculator copy + paste** — long-press the display copies; a paste
   chip beside the copy chip sanitizes the clipboard into a valid
   expression and lands it like a memory recall.
5. **Recents two tabs + filter tabs + nav reset** — owe chips split from
   the direction filter (they used to share one state var), the filter row
   appears under the active owe tab, both compose, and the Recents nav
   button resets everything (new UiStore.recentsResetTick).
6. **Post-call refresh** — debounced ContentObserver on the system call
   log refreshes Recents ~1.2s after the provider writes the ended call,
   before the user returns.
7. **Lighter call cards** — flat canvas rows with an inset hairline instead
   of filled/bordered cards; smaller avatar ring, lighter type, brand-soft
   call disc; canvas-toned swipe underlay.
